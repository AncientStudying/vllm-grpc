Pinned inline at spec level per the M6.1.3 inline-threshold-pinning precedent — removes the deferred-knob from `/speckit-plan`. CI half-width definition for `wall_p50_ms` is the standard ±1.96 × stderr (95% normal approximation) consistent with M6.1.x's published CIs.

### Session 2026-05-19 (round 4 — exogenous time-varying confounds over the 20-40h sweep)

- Q: Sweep iteration / cohort-interleaving strategy for the 20-40h publish sweep — cohort-innermost block iteration, cohort-innermost RPC-level interleaving, randomized point order, or cohort-outermost block iteration? → A: **Cohort-innermost block iteration (Option A)**. For each `(cell, max_tokens)` tuple the sweep orchestrator MUST run all `n` RPCs for cohort 1, then cohort 2, then cohort 3, then cohort 4, BEFORE moving to the next `(cell, max_tokens)` tuple — making **cohort the innermost iteration variable**. All four cohorts at a given `(cell, max_tokens)` block share a ~30 min – 4 h time window (cell-dependent: ~30 min at low-cap × c=1, ~4 h at high-cap × c=1), so between-cohort `wall_p50_ms` comparisons (User Story 1 budget table; User Story 2 crossover analysis) are time-of-day-controlled at the granularity that matters for the headline cohort-vs-cohort claim. Within a cohort, the `n` RPCs are drawn from a contiguous block (~tens of minutes to ~1 h depending on cell), so within-cohort stddev remains coherent and matches the validate-mode round-3 variance-gate measurement (FR-004). Channel setup/teardown amortizes across the `n` RPCs per cohort block. The `(cell × max_tokens)` outer loop order is implementation-deferred to `/speckit-plan` but MUST ensure each `(cell, max_tokens)` tuple's 4 cohort blocks run contiguously. Captured in new FR-030; round-4 introduction (this clarification round is dedicated to exogenous confounds that the spec previously did not address).
- Q: Mid-sweep null-anchor re-measurement cadence — periodic light re-anchoring at every 4h mark, start+end only, no mid-sweep re-anchoring, or RPC-level canary every 15-30 min? → A: **Periodic light re-anchoring at every 4h mark (Option A)**. The cohort-innermost interleaving from FR-030 controls *between-cohort* time-of-day bias within each `(cell, max_tokens)` block, but it does NOT detect *intra-sweep latency drift* (the M6.1.3-baseline anchors at sweep start vs sweep end may differ purely due to congestion variance over the 20-40h window — drift that the cross-milestone FR-012 / FR-013 / SC-004 anchor comparison against M6.1.3's published CIs would silently absorb). New FR-031: at every 4h wall-clock mark, the orchestrator runs a lightweight anchor block per cohort — `chat_stream × c=1 × max_tokens=10`, `n=20` RPCs per cohort (~30s per cohort × 4 cohorts = ~2 min total per 4h mark; ≤ ~25 min cumulative overhead over a 40h sweep, < 1.5% of the wall-clock budget). The reporter aggregates these into an **anchor-latency trajectory** (one trajectory per cohort, 8-10 snapshots in publish mode) and fires a `latency_drift_warning` line per cohort if the trajectory's max-minus-min `wall_p50_ms` spread exceeds the M6.1.3-published CI half-width for that cohort at chat_stream c=1 × max_tokens=10. Aligns with the existing FR-009 `network_paths` 4h cadence — a unified "sweep health check" pulse covering both topology drift AND latency drift. Validate-mode (< 8h wall-clock) runs start + end only (the 4h cadence rule MAY skip in-flight re-anchors if total sweep wall-clock < 8h). Captured in new FR-031; new AnchorLatencyTrajectory entity; new SC-015 spelling out the trajectory contract.
- Q: Sweep-level escalation when intra-sweep latency drift is detected — operator-decides with dedicated sweep-level integrity header at ≥ 2-of-4 cohorts drifted, auto-caveat per-cohort verdicts, block publication when drift exceeds 2× CI half-width, or quiet per-cohort warning only? → A: **Operator-decides + dedicated sweep-level integrity header at ≥ 2-of-4 cohorts drifted (Option A)**. The published markdown gains a FOURTH sweep-level integrity warning channel (distinct from FR-014's null-anchor cross-milestone header, FR-029's failure-summary header, and FR-009 / SC-010's cohort-CSP-mismatch warning): a leading callout fires when ≥ 2 of the 4 cohorts show `latency_drift_warning = true` from FR-031's anchor-latency trajectory. The 2-of-4 threshold gives one-cohort-of-headroom (a single isolated cohort drift, which would likely indicate a cohort-specific topology event already covered by FR-009 / SC-010, does NOT trigger the sweep-level header; systematic multi-cohort drift — region-level congestion variance — does). Publication is NOT blocked; the operator reads the header + per-cohort lines and decides whether to publish or rerun against a fresh Modal deploy at a different time-of-day. Validate-mode applies the same 2-of-4 rule against its 2-snapshot start+end trajectory. FR-031 amended to spell out the integrity-header firing rule; new SC-016 spelling out the firing contract.
- Q: Per-block UTC timestamping for iteration-discipline verification + post-hoc time-of-day attribution — per-block timestamps in JSON + "Sweep wall-clock timeline" subsection in markdown + `iteration_discipline_verified` boolean, JSON-only timestamps, hour-band enum only, or no per-block timestamping? → A: **JSON timestamps + markdown wall-clock timeline subsection + `iteration_discipline_verified` machine check (Option A)**. Each row in the latency budget table's JSON gains additive `block_start_utc` and `block_end_utc` fields (strict-superset per FR-011; M6.1.x-aware readers ignore the new fields unchanged). The published markdown gains a compact "Sweep wall-clock timeline" subsection at the bottom of the artifact rendering one row per `(cell, max_tokens)` tuple showing each cohort's block start UTC time and duration in minutes — so an operator can VISUALLY verify the FR-030 cohort-innermost discipline (the 4 cohorts at each tuple SHOULD form a contiguous time band). The reporter ALSO computes `iteration_discipline_verified: bool` in `run_meta` — true iff every `(cell, max_tokens)` tuple's 4 cohort blocks ran contiguously without interleaving from other tuples; false signals an iteration-order break and emits a warning at the artifact header (a 5th sweep-level integrity channel, but a soft one — discipline-verified is purely diagnostic, not blocking). M7/M8 readers can segment the budget table by UTC hour-band post-hoc from the per-block timestamps. Validate mode includes per-block timestamps but renders the wall-clock timeline subsection only if the validate sweep ran ≥ 8h (otherwise omits as low-signal). New FR-032; new SC-017; FR-016 amended to include the new subsection; SC-008 amended to list the new `iteration_discipline_verified` and timestamp fields.
- Q: Failed-block re-measurement policy under FR-030 — in-window retry once with no after-the-fact retries, no retries (first failure is final), end-of-sweep retry pass, or defer to `/speckit-plan`? → A: **In-window retry once, no after-the-fact retries (Option A)**. Each `(cell, cohort, max_tokens)` block MAY retry ONCE if the first attempt fails (transient gRPC error, dispatch timeout, single-RPC OOM that doesn't crash the engine, etc.) BEFORE the orchestrator advances past the current `(cell, max_tokens)` tuple — the retry stays in the same FR-030 time-of-day window as the 3 sibling cohorts at that tuple, so cohort comparison is not confounded. If the retry also fails, the block is marked `failed_<reason>` per FR-029 and the orchestrator moves on; the failure-summary subsection counts the block; SC-014's firing rules apply. Retries AFTER the orchestrator has advanced past a `(cell, max_tokens)` tuple are FORBIDDEN — they would silently violate FR-030 (the retry runs in a different time-of-day window than its 3 sibling cohorts, confounding cohort comparison for that tuple). No end-of-sweep retry pass. This requirement is DISTINCT from FR-026's Modal-deploy-level preemption recovery (which operates at resume-the-whole-sweep granularity and is bound to the cohort-block boundary it was preempted at). New FR-033; FR-029 amended to reference the retry policy.

### Session 2026-05-19 (round 5 — prompt source, EOS handling, and KV-pressure sub-probe)

**Context for round 5**: Code-surface investigation during `/speckit-plan` revealed that the M6.x family currently uses **synthetic seed-derived prompts** (`_build_chat_prompt(seed) → "M6 chat probe seed={N} digest={X}. Please respond."` in `m6_rpc_driver.py:106-109` and `torch.randn` random tensors for embed in `m6_1_rpc_driver.py:113-131`), NOT corpus-sourced prompts. `ignore_eos` is not set anywhere in the codebase, so the engine samples EOS naturally — and on the short synthetic probe, Qwen3-8B reliably emits EOS within ~50-200 tokens regardless of the `max_tokens` cap. This breaks two M6.2 spec claims silently: (a) the `max_tokens=2048` row in the budget table doesn't reflect 2048-token generation; (b) FR-017a's wall-clock-ratio inference threshold (2.2) is meaningless if both `c=8 × max_tokens=1024` and `c=8 × max_tokens=2048` measurements terminate at natural EOS far below the cap. Round 5 pins the resolution: **Option D three-regime split** — synthetic prompts at null anchors (preserves FR-012 / FR-013 baseline comparison), corpus prompts at interior caps (production-realistic latency), `ignore_eos=True` sub-probe at `c=8 × {1024, 2048}` for Story 3 KV-pressure characterization.

- Q: Corpus choice for chat cells (interior `max_tokens` caps `{256, 512, 1024, 2048}` in publish; `{2048}` in validate) — existing ShareGPT, new long-eliciting corpus, or blended? → A: **Existing ShareGPT corpus `tools/benchmark/corpus/chat_sharegpt_1000.json` (Option A)**. SHA-pinned in `chat_sharegpt_1000.provenance.json` (`corpus_sha256: 4442302df439fdc1967e9fb48a88910cee5d0f712592e733d47bdbbc1e0374f1`); 1000 first-human-turn prompts, 561 short / 324 medium / 115 long buckets per the provenance manifest; M5.2-vintage, already in the repo. Loaded via existing `corpus.load_corpus(DEFAULT_CHAT_CORPUS_PATH)` machinery (no new corpus engineering). Per-RPC prompt selection uses the existing-but-currently-unused `symmetric_prompts.assign_symmetric_prompt(iter_idx, cohort, corpus)` helper (cohort-invariant by design) so every cohort sees the same prompt at the same iteration index — the cohort-symmetry property M6.1.x got from synthetic-seed-determinism is preserved through the corpus mode. The short-bucket's tendency to elicit very short natural completions (often 1-2 sentences regardless of cap) is a known property the artifact narrative documents; it doesn't break Story 1's interpretation because "production latency at this cap with naturalistic prompts" is the answer Story 1 is asking. The `corpus_sha256` and `corpus_path` are persisted in `run_meta` for audit reproducibility. New FR-034; the per-row `prompt_source` field gains a new value `"corpus_sharegpt"` for interior-cap rows.

The existing `tools/benchmark/corpus/completions_embeds/` corpus is a 20-entry `seq_len × 1024` corpus — incompatible with M6.1.x's Qwen3-8B (hidden_size=4096) and insufficient in volume for M6.2's RPC scales. Per-RPC prompt selection uses the same `symmetric_prompts.assign_symmetric_prompt(iter_idx, cohort, embed_corpus)` helper so embed cohorts are cohort-symmetric per iteration index. **Three-regime split for embed cells** mirrors the chat-cell three-regime split: (i) null-anchor embed cells at `max_tokens ∈ {10, 50}` use per-RPC random tensor synthesis via the existing `m6_1_rpc_driver.build_torch_save_bytes(rpc_index, base_seed)` machinery — preserves the M6.1.3 published embed-cell CIs byte-for-byte at the null anchors, FR-012 / FR-013 null-anchor comparison is unchanged; (ii) interior-cap embed cells at `max_tokens ∈ {256, 512, 1024, 2048}` use the new ShareGPT-derived embed corpus — production-realistic, methodologically consistent with the chat-cell regime that also uses ShareGPT prompts; (iii) KV-pressure embed sub-probe at `c=8 × {1024, 2048}` uses ShareGPT-derived embeddings (same as interior-cap) — `ignore_eos=True` forces generation to cap regardless of prompt semantics so either regime would work, but ShareGPT-derived keeps the sub-probe consistent with its sibling interior-cap measurements. The `corpus_sha256_embed` and `corpus_path_embed` are persisted in `run_meta` for audit reproducibility. New FR-035; the per-row `prompt_source` field for embed rows takes one of `{"synthetic_random_tensor", "corpus_sharegpt_embed"}`.
- Q: KV-pressure sub-probe concurrency scope — `c=8` only, `c=1` and `c=8`, or all concurrencies `c ∈ {1, 4, 8}`? → A: **`c=8` only (Option A)**. Matches FR-017a's spec definition exactly: the wall-clock-ratio inference `R = wall_p50_ms(c=8, max_tokens=2048) / wall_p50_ms(c=8, max_tokens=1024)` is the spec-pinned signal. The sub-probe runs `4 cohorts × 2 cell-types {chat_stream, embed} × 2 caps {1024, 2048} × n RPCs` = 16 sub-probe blocks. At c=8 × max_tokens=2048 the KV-cache occupancy is `8 × 2048 = 16,384` tokens against the ~31K-token engine ceiling — half-headroom, close to capacity but not OOM — the half-budget reference cited in User Story 3. At c=1 or c=4 the KV pressure is far below the ceiling and the wall-clock-ratio is trivially ~2.0 (linear scaling with cap, no concurrency-induced KV bottleneck); adding those concurrencies would add wall-clock cost without delivering KV-pressure information. Sub-probe stays focused on the regime FR-017a defines. New FR-036 (sub-probe scope + orchestration); KVPressureObservation entity unchanged — the sub-probe populates the same per-cohort × per-cell-type record FR-017 already specifies.
- Q: KV-pressure sub-probe `n` (per-block RPC count) — `n=20` lightweight, `n=50` medium, or same as round-3-pinned main-sweep n? → A: **`n=20` lightweight (Option A)**. Pinned in spec; no `/speckit-plan` deferral. The wall-clock-ratio inference is a ratio of two p50 measurements at the same cohort × cell-type; ratios cancel common multiplicative noise, so the inference is robust to small-n. Total sub-probe RPCs: 4 cohorts × 2 cell-types × 2 caps × n=20 = 320 RPCs; ~30 min – 1 h cumulative wall-clock at chat_stream c=8 × max_tokens=2048 (each RPC is ~8-9 s with concurrent dispatch amortization). < 2% of the publish sweep wall-clock budget regardless of round-3 main-sweep n selection. ### Key Entities

- **MeasurementPoint**: A single (cell, cohort, `max_tokens`) tuple — the atomic measurement unit. Carries `n=20` per-RPC timing rows in validate mode; publish-mode `n` is the round-3-pinned `n=40` (`m6_2_types.M6_2_PUBLISH_N`) per FR-004. Defining attributes: `cell_id` (one of `embed_c1` / `embed_c4` / `embed_c8` / `chat_stream_c1` / `chat_stream_c4` / `chat_stream_c8`), `cohort` (one of the four), `max_tokens` (one of `{10, 50, 256, 512, 1024, 2048}`), `n_rpcs`, `wall_p50_ms`, `wall_p95_ms`, `wall_p99_ms`, `tpot_ms` (chat_stream only), the inherited 5-segment decomposition. **Round-5 additions**: `prompt_source: Literal["synthetic_seed_derived", "corpus_sharegpt", "synthetic_random_tensor", "corpus_sharegpt_embed"]` per FR-034 / FR-035 — null-anchor rows carry the synthetic value, interior-cap rows carry the corpus value, sub-probe rows carry the corpus value matching the cell-type; `measurement_regime: Literal["natural_eos", "forced_cap_ignore_eos_true"]` per FR-036 — budget-table rows carry `natural_eos`, KV-pressure sub-probe rows carry `forced_cap_ignore_eos_true`; per-RPC `prompt_corpus_idx: int | None` for corpus-regime rows (the iteration index passed to `assign_symmetric_prompt`, `None` for synthetic regimes) so post-hoc analysis can re-derive which corpus entry drove each RPC.
- **NullAnchor**: A MeasurementPoint at `max_tokens ∈ {10, 50}` paired with the corresponding M6.1.3 published measurement IF cross-checkable (22 cells); otherwise paired with a `new_baseline_marker = true` flag (26 cells). Cross-checkable cells carry a drift verdict (`PASS` / `WARN` / `FAIL`) and the CI-overlap statistic that produced the verdict; new-baseline cells carry `drift_verdict = null` + `new_baseline_marker = true`.
- **CrossoverThreshold**: Per-cell record capturing the smallest `max_tokens` at which the M6.1.3 cohort-pair winner's `wall_p50_ms` and the second-place cohort's `wall_p50_ms` satisfy the symmetric mean-in-CI rule (either mean falls inside the other cohort's `[wall_p50_ms ± CI_half_width]` interval at 95% CI). Carries `crossover_max_tokens`, `crossover_evidence` (which cohort-direction triggered the rule, the two cohorts' `wall_p50_ms` and CI half-widths at the crossover axis point), and `m6_1_3_base_verdict`.
- **KVPressureObservation**: Single record for the `c=8 × max_tokens=2048` regime, computed per cohort × cell-type ∈ {chat_stream, embed} from the FR-036 KV-pressure **sub-probe** (NOT from the main-sweep budget-table rows; the sub-probe sets `ignore_eos=True` so the engine generates to the cap on every RPC, which is the measurement FR-017a's wall-clock-ratio inference + 2.2 threshold are calibrated against). Carries `kv_cache_used_fraction_peak` (best-effort engine field; `null` if unavailable), `wall_clock_ratio_c8_2048_over_1024` (REQUIRED; per FR-017a, computed from sub-probe `wall_p50_ms`), `wall_clock_inference_label` (one of `kv_pressure_inferred_<chat_stream|embed>` / `kv_pressure_not_observable`), `scheduling_stall_signals` (free-form notes from vLLM engine logs, if any), `oom_observed` boolean, `sub_probe_n_rpcs: int = 20` (per FR-036 / round-5 Q4 pinned), `sub_probe_prompt_source: Literal["corpus_sharegpt", "corpus_sharegpt_embed"]` (the corpus regime used; chat sub-probe uses `corpus_sharegpt`, embed sub-probe uses `corpus_sharegpt_embed`).
- **NetworkPathSnapshot**: Inherited from M6.1.2. Carries `endpoint_ip`, ordered `hops` list, per-hop CSP annotation, cohort `cloud_provider` enum, `region`, `snapshot_timestamp`. M6.2 captures a trajectory of these (multiple snapshots over the ~30–40 hour wall-clock) rather than a single observation.
- **AnchorLatencyTrajectory**: Per-cohort sequence of intra-sweep re-anchor snapshots (per FR-031). Each snapshot is a lightweight measurement of `chat_stream × c=1 × max_tokens=10` at `n=20`, captured at sweep start + end + every 4h mark in publish mode (8-10 snapshots) and at sweep start + end only in validate mode (2 snapshots). Carries per-snapshot `wall_p50_ms`, `wall_p95_ms`, `wall_p99_ms`, and `snapshot_timestamp`; per-cohort trajectory carries the derived `max_minus_min_wall_p50_ms` spread and a `latency_drift_warning` boolean firing when the spread exceeds the M6.1.3-published CI half-width for that cohort at chat_stream c=1 × max_tokens=10. Distinct from NullAnchor (which is cross-milestone, comparing M6.2 measurements to M6.1.3's published CIs) — AnchorLatencyTrajectory characterizes intra-M6.2-sweep drift only.

## Assumptions

- **Hardware and engine config unchanged from M6.1 / M6.1.1 / M6.1.2 / M6.1.3.** Modal A10G eu-west-1 deploy, Qwen3-8B fp16, real prompt-embeds engine path, `max_model_len=2048`, concurrent dispatch. Any deviation invalidates the null-anchor comparison and would force re-baselining the entire M6.x family.
- **M6.1.3's published artifact is the canonical null-anchor reference.** PR #31 (2026-05-19) is the latest closed M6.1.x milestone; its `docs/benchmarks/m6_1_3-attribution-closure.json` is the spec-level source of truth for `max_tokens=10 / 50` anchor comparison. If M6.1.3's artifact is itself superseded mid-cycle (e.g., an M6.1.4 lands during M6.2 spec authoring), the reference MUST be updated in `/speckit-clarify` and FR-013 amended accordingly.
- **Symmetric prompts default on per M6.1.3's cross-milestone recommendation.** The `symmetric_prompts` helper (originally landed in M6.1.3) is the M6.x convention going forward; M6.2 inherits unchanged. M6.2 does NOT ship an asymmetric-prompts CLI override (forward-referenced for a future milestone per FR-008); the published artifact is symmetric-prompts by default and the only mode supported via the milestone CLI.
- **4-cohort matrix per M6.1.2 reintroduction of `rest_plain_tcp`.** PLAN.md's M6.2 section (written before M6.1.2 landed) projected 3 cohorts × 6 caps × 6 cells = 108 measurement points; the current 4-cohort convention yields 144 measurement points (~33% more RPCs, ~33% more wall-clock, ~33% more Modal cost). FR-005, FR-021, FR-023 carry the recalibrated numbers.
- **Single-run-per-point in M6.2 publish mode; publish-mode `n` deferred to clarify round 3.** M6.2 does NOT inherit M6.1.3's `--m6_1_3-diagnose-repeat` multi-run convention; instead the per-point `n` is tuned to the validate-sweep variance signal. The publish-mode `n` (uniform-100, adaptive-100/50, or uniform-50) is pinned in clarify round 3 against measured within-cohort stddev at chat_stream c=1 × max_tokens=2048 per FR-004. Wall-clock and Modal-spend caps (FR-021 / FR-023 / SC-001) carry provisional ranges until round 3 closes. If validate-sweep data also reveals high between-run variance, a `--m6_2-repeat=N` flag MAY additionally be added in round 3.
- **Wall-clock budget assumes M6.1.3 TPOT scales linearly.** The round-3-provisional wall-clock budgets (≤ 24-48 h depending on `n` per FR-023) assume ~33.7 ms/token TPOT (M6.1's measured rate at h=4096) holds across the axis. If validate-sweep TPOT at `max_tokens=2048` deviates materially (>20%) from M6.1's per-token rate, the publish-sweep wall-clock and cost bounds MUST be revised in round 3 alongside the `n` selection.
- **KV-cache budget is approximately ~31K tokens.** The half-headroom claim for `c=8 × max_tokens=2048` derives from this assumption; if vLLM's actual KV budget at the chosen engine config differs (e.g., due to fp16 vs fp8 KV cache, `gpu_memory_utilization` setting), the SC-006 narrative MUST cite the actual measured budget rather than the projected 31K.
- **No upstream vLLM contributions or engine-config probes required.** M6.2 reuses the M6.1.3 engine setup verbatim; the new `max_tokens` axis is a client-side sweep-orchestrator change only. Engine-side `engine_kv_cache_used_fraction` instrumentation via gRPC trailing metadata is best-effort — if vLLM does not expose the field, FR-017 / SC-006 still characterize the KV-pressure regime via the REQUIRED wall-clock-ratio inference (FR-017a). The wall-clock signal is the canonical answer for User Story 3; the engine field cross-validates when available.
- **Sweep mode persisted in artifact.** The `run_meta` block MUST record `sweep_mode = "m6_2_publish"` or `"m6_2_validate"` (strict-superset addition; no `schema_version` bump) so post-hoc consumers can distinguish the two modes' artifacts without inspecting the filename.
- **Corpus methodology — what the M6.2 measurements represent.** The chat and embed corpora are derived from `chat_sharegpt_1000.json` — 1000 first-human-turn prompts sampled from the ShareGPT conversation dataset, filtered to `4 ≤ char_count ≤ 2048`. ShareGPT itself is a corpus of logged ChatGPT conversations from real users; using its first-human-turn slice means the M6.2 measurements characterize **how a serving system handles the distribution of first-turn conversational prompts that real chat assistants receive**, with Qwen3-8B tokenization. Under this tokenizer the realized distribution is heavily left-skewed (median 18 tokens, p90 128, p99 479, max 809; ~2.4% of entries are 1-token follow-ups like `"continue"` / `"next"` that are themselves real interaction patterns). The cohort comparisons (chat vs embed transport, REST vs gRPC, plain-TCP vs HTTPS-edge) are methodologically clean across this distribution — all cohorts see byte-identical prompts at byte-identical iteration indices via `symmetric_prompts.assign_symmetric_prompt`. The artifact narrative MUST NOT generalize beyond first-turn conversational traffic without explicit caveats: agentic / RAG / code / multi-turn-with-rolling-context workloads have systematically longer prefills and are not represented in this corpus. Stratification by prompt length is supported via the per-row `prompt_corpus_idx` (the `iter_idx` that selected the prompt), which lets post-hoc readers join against the manifest's `seq_len` field rather than relying on the bucket label. The `bucket` field is informative-only and recalibrated 2026-05-21 to the realized distribution (`< 16 → short`, `16–127 → medium`, `≥ 128 → long`) giving roughly 44 / 46 / 10% splits; the earlier `< 128 → short` thresholds put 90% of entries in `short` and were not useful as a stratifier.
